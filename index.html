What happened

AI Hardware Derates Ahead of Nvidia's Post-Huang Test

Wall Street retreated on Monday as the S&P 500 fell 1.95% to 7,414.41, the Nasdaq sank 2.52% to 24,503.07, and the VIX jumped 21.35% to 23.42. Semiconductors plunged 5.12%, with 61% of institutional SMH options money in puts, as cut AI capex forecasts and a Trump administration move to close a China advanced-chip loophole hit Micron (down 7.62%), AMD (down 5.58%), and Nvidia (down 4.12% to $156.14). Leveraged AI data-center builders such as Applied Digital (down 8.70%) fell alongside them as JPMorgan credit analysts warned new deals could take longer and Anthropic prepared to price its IPO on Wednesday at a reported $1.4 trillion valuation.

Interim CEO Colette Kress leads Nvidia's fiscal Q3 report after Tuesday's US close against $108 billion revenue guidance and $109.3 billion Visible Alpha consensus. While the Financial Times reported Silicon Valley allies formed a war room to back Jensen Huang's return after he resigned over a reported relationship with a suspected Chinese intelligence tie, CNBC cited a source saying the board is not considering his return, and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent urged an outside CEO shake-up. Kalshi prices Huang returning as next CEO at 8% (down 6 points), Arm chief Rene Haas at 29% (up 5 points), and a permanent CEO announcement before December 1 at 4%.

Consumer Staples (up 0.27%) and Utilities (up 0.18%) were the only positive US sectors tracked on Monday. BTIG noted that consumer staples have beaten tech by 33%, the widest performance gap in roughly 25 years.

Sources Cointelegraph, Benzinga, Moomoo, CNBC, Kalshi, Crypto Briefing, Boise Dev

Sticky Yields and Hawkish Fed Bets Sink Precious Metals

US Treasuries caught only a small haven bid during the equity sell-off, pulling the 10-year yield down 2.0 basis points to 5.133% and the 2-year yield down 3.8 basis points to 4.836% while German Bund yields ticked higher. Speaking in San Francisco, Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh suggested AI's economic boost might disappoint while steering clear of inflation risk, whereas Kansas City Fed President Jeff Schmid said policymakers still have work to do on inflation.

Kalshi traders price a quarter-point December Fed rate hike at 90% and a larger hike at 6% (down 5 points), even as falling retail fuel costs pulled odds of November year-over-year CPI topping 3.7% down 23 points to 46% ahead of Wednesday's October FOMC minutes. Elevated real yields and a 0.18% rise in the dollar index to 103.60 pushed gold down 0.81% to a one-year low of $3,855.00, leaving bullion down 15% in 12 weeks and on track for its worst year since 2015, while silver dropped 4.46% to $46.93 amid falling bar and coin demand.

Sources Cointelegraph, WSJ, CNBC, Reuters, Boston Herald, Kitco, BullionVault, Kalshi

Asian Chip Markets Tumble as Mainland China Data Misses

Asian chip hubs absorbed steep losses on Monday ahead of Nvidia's print. Taiwan's benchmark plunged 6.03% to 41,040.08 as TSMC slid 5.74% despite a 59.7% jump in October sales, South Korea's KOSPI slumped more than 3% on heavy declines in Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix, and Japan's Nikkei 225 lost 2.42% to 59,662.02 as weak GDP figures compounded double-digit drops in chip-equipment makers Advantest and Lasertec. Singapore's STI fell 0.93% to 5,183.18, dragged down by a 2.68% slide in Singtel after its group CEO sold US$6.3 million in shares.

Mainland China's Shanghai Composite declined 0.87% to 3,602.57 after October factory output, retail sales, and home prices missed expectations across the board, indicating recent stimulus has yet to gain traction. Even so, Hong Kong's Hang Seng Index rose 0.49% to 23,753.84 on strength in domestic restaurant and home-appliance shares, India's Nifty 50 gained 0.58% to 25,291.10 as cheaper oil supported importers, and Europe's STOXX 600 dipped 0.28% to 662.31 as tech and miners lagged firmer defense and consumer stocks.

Sources Cointelegraph, TradingView, TradingKey, The Business Times, Bloomberg.com, Reuters, KELO-AM, Devdiscourse, Moomoo, VnExpress International

Oil and Natural Gas Slide on Surplus Supply and Mild Weather

Crude and gas benchmarks fell sharply on Monday, pulling the S&P Energy sector down 1.96%. WTI crude retreated 3.42% to $85.53 and Brent crude lost 3.65% to $92.34 as recovering Middle East exports and fading Iran risk premiums refocused traders on global supply-surplus forecasts. US retail gasoline prices have dropped to multi-month lows in several states, and Kalshi traders slashed odds of November US retail gas averaging above $3.60 by 35 points to 34% while pricing just a 10% chance that WTI finishes Tuesday above $86.49.

US natural gas futures declined 3.10% to $3.13 as a brief cold snap faded, mild weather forecasts curbed heating demand, domestic production recovered, and easing West Texas pipeline bottlenecks lifted negative Waha cash prices to a 16-week high.

Sources WSJ, Reuters, Oil & Gas 360, Michigan Farm News, The Detroit News, Natural Gas Intelligence, EnergyNow.com, Kalshi

Crypto Stalls Under ETF Outflows Despite Institutional Plumbing Wins

Bitcoin edged down 0.16% to $65,027.13 and Ether slipped 0.32% to $1,820.16 after weekend liquidations briefly sent Bitcoin to $61,950—below its 200-week moving average near $68,186—with daily RSI at 20. November spot Bitcoin ETF outflows hit $2.1 billion, skewing options toward $60,000 puts ahead of Nvidia's report, though Kalshi prices an 88% chance Bitcoin holds at or above $60,000 Friday afternoon. Corporate treasuries bought the dip: Bitmine added 7,653 ETH to reach 4.97% of Ether supply, and Strategy bought 485 BTC for $33.4 million while spending $228 million on STRC preferred-share buybacks ahead of its November 30 MSCI removal.

Major financial institutions announced new blockchain rails despite weak token prices: DBS and JPMorgan's Kinexys launched 24/7 cross-border tokenized dollar deposit transfers between Singapore and the US, Coinbase and Broadridge proposed an Onchain Rights Standard for tokenized stock notices, and Zelle selected Solana for its ZelleUSD international stablecoin as Solana dropped its six-year beta label. Solana nonetheless slipped 0.72% to a four-month low of $71.39 as SOL ETF outflows stretched to six consecutive weeks with crypto Fear and Greed at 12.

Sources Cointelegraph, Bloomberg.com, CoinDesk, CoinGape, Cryptonews.net, Crypto News, Kalshi
