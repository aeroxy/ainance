What happened

Hot jobs lift yields and the dollar

A stronger October payrolls print of 165k pushed Treasury yields back up, with the 10-year at 4.86% up 2.2 basis points and the 2-year at 4.51% up 3.5 basis points, and the dollar index up 0.72% to 102.28. The move re-priced higher-for-longer odds and pressured rate-sensitive assets into the close.

Gold slipped 0.83% to 4,079 on the day even as it headed for its best week since August after midterms, while silver eased 0.78% as Goldman cut gold forecasts on hawkish Fed demand and the firmer dollar weighed.

Sources Finimize, Vietnam.vn, Advisor Perspectives, Forbes, Bloomberg.com, Yahoo Finance

US bounces after Asia breaks

Wall Street staged a fragile bounce with the S&P rising 0.31% to 7,376 and Nasdaq up 1.09% to 24,579, while the Dow fell 0.46% to 51,285 and VIX eased 9% to 25.64 but stayed elevated. Semis led with a 3.62% gain after an 11.5% crash, with chip names among top market-cap movers and AI stocks halting their worst rout since 2020.

Asia bore the brunt Friday, with Nikkei down 5.96% into bear market, KOSPI down 6.02% triggering a circuit breaker, Taiwan down 4.51% and Hang Seng off 1.27%, part of a $1.2 trillion regional rout. Europe followed with STOXX 600 down 1.00% before a late steadying.

Sources Yahoo Finance, Southern Farm Network, The Business Times, Investing.com, Trefis, www.tokenpost.com, KuCoin, Business Recorder, BusinessToday Malaysia, ad-hoc-news.de

Crypto bleeds on ETF exits

Crypto stayed soft as spot Bitcoin ETFs shed $1.3 billion in a week, flipping negative for 2026 after a $1.28 billion outflow week, the worst since June, and Ether logged a sixth week of outflows. Bitcoin held near 75,693 down 0.25% and Ether near 2,229 flat, with Solana losing key support post-upgrade and XRP seeing zero inflows.

The outflow regime kept beta under pressure even as equities bounced, with the $75k Bitcoin level in focus for the next leg and analysts flagging risks of a deeper pull toward $67k if selling persists.

Sources 24/7 Wall St., The Block, CryptoTicker.io, TradingView, Benzinga, CoinGape, Crypto News

Oil soft, gas firm

Oil eased with WTI at 90.34 down 0.20% and Brent near 98.32 as traders weighed midterm signals for an end to the Iran war and a temporary US easing of Russia oil sanctions, including a Russian diesel tanker reportedly headed for US shores. Brent was set for its biggest weekly drop since 2020 despite earlier war-premium spikes.

Natural gas diverged, rising to 3.00 up 1.90% as the 200-day average held and physical weekend prices powered up with Waha turning positive for the first time since February, offsetting a 4% weekly dip on record output and steady LNG export demand.

Sources Oil & Gas Journal, EnergyNow, CNN, Alaska Story, Euronext Markets: Real-time Stock Market Data, FXEmpire, Natural Gas Intelligence, EnergyNow.com
