What happened

Macro and rates

October CPI came in hotter than expected, pushing the 10-year Treasury up 12.6 basis points to 4.99% and the 2-year up 18.4 basis points to 4.73%, with the dollar index up 0.6% to 102.25. Fed officials opened the door to a half-point hike and Treasury yields raced back to 5% on expectations of higher rates.

The S&P 500 eased 0.4% to 7,520 and the Nasdaq fell 0.8% to 25,063 after a prior rebound, while the VIX eased to 19.6. European shares held near record highs as lower oil prices boosted sentiment, with the STOXX 600 up 0.3%, even as Wall Street retreated on rate worries.

Sources PBS, Reuters, The Washington Post, WSJ, CNBC, www.thenationalnews.com, Beinsure, MarketWatch

US equities diverge

US equities were mixed to lower with tech leading the retreat, semis down 0.8% and utilities and real estate soft as yields jumped. Nvidia sank about 6% for a second day as investors weighed a board probe into the CEO's affair with an alleged spy and Bank of America removed it from top picks with target cuts.

Winners were stock-specific. Take-Two jumped more than 8% with 2.4 times volume after reiterating GTA VI for November 19, and data-center linked names surged — AAON up 19.3% on a huge beat and higher guidance and Csquare up 14.7% on a revenue beat and raised outlook. Cisco was in focus ahead of results that have moved the stock 8% or more three times running.

Sources Benzinga, Reuters, MarketWatch, Investment Week, Yahoo Finance, 24/7 Wall St., Variety, Investor's Business Daily, Moomoo, kalkine.ca, MarketBeat

Commodities split

Oil extended declines to the lowest since August after Trump said the Iran war is effectively over and further strikes are unnecessary, with WTI down 0.5% to 86.38 and Brent down 0.7% to 91.96 and briefly below 90. European gas prices also dipped as war escalation was downplayed, though distillate tightness offered some support.

Gold fell 1.8% to 4,041 and silver 2.2% to 51.55 as the CPI beat lifted the dollar and yields, with bullion down nearly 3% intraday. Natural gas bucked the move, up 2.8% to 3.31 on short-covering, colder forecasts and reduced output, extending winning ways even as an EIA winter forecast called for more gas and less cold.

Sources 24/7 Wall St., WSJ, CNBC, Bloomberg.com, Crude Oil Prices Today, Reuters, Kitco, FXEmpire, Natural Gas Intelligence, Barchart.com

Crypto under pressure

Bitcoin dropped 1.5% to 73,780 near a three-month low as hot US inflation dimmed risk appetite. The move came with heavy ETF outflows — 201 million in a day and 1.66 billion over seven sessions — quickly rising as fear peaked.

Ether fell 3.3% to 2,059 on a 5 to 6% leverage flush and 184.6 million in daily ETF redemptions led by BlackRock. Solana fell 3.8% to 83.31 and XRP fell 3.2% to 1.06, down 14% on the week, as alt beta amplified the move and XRP ETFs bled 10.4 million in a day.

Sources Bloomberg.com, Yahoo Finance, Santiment, Cryptonews.net, Binance, CoinMarketCap, TradingView, 24/7 Wall St.
