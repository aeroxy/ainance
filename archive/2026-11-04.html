What happened

Yields climb into the vote

Treasury yields pushed toward 5% with the 10-year up 6.2bp to 4.956% and the 2-year up 3.8bp to 4.621% on strong jobs-openings data and midterm polling. The dollar index edged up 0.11% to 101.86 and USD/JPY rose 0.48% to 158.70, pressuring havens.

The VIX fell to 20.73, down about 10% in two days, as risk-on bounced. Markets now watch JOLTS, refunding and the vote for the next rate and tariff cue.

Sources The Economic Times, Bloomberg.com, WSJ, CNBC, Yahoo Finance

US: Tech rebound offsets Dow drag

The S&P rose 0.36% to 7,568.62 and Nasdaq rose 0.84% to 25,375 while the Dow fell 0.41% to 51,770 as Apple sank after earnings and tech led. Semis jumped 2.01% and tech added 1.33%, with staples down 2.05% and materials down 2.07%.

Shopify tumbled 8.74% on heavy volume after Q3 revenue and operating profit missed, while AppLovin surged 7.28% ahead of earnings as ad-tech rebounded. Chip names led the bounce with Nvidia up 2.27% and AMD up 3.65% into its report.

Sources Yahoo Finance, Yahoo Finance Singapore, Stock Titan, 24/7 Wall St., MarketWatch, CNBC

Asia mixed, Europe soft

Europe slipped with the STOXX 600 down 0.58% as tech fell about 2% and Middle East tensions weighed, and London closed lower as US voting began. Asia was mixed: Taiwan fell 3.59% leading chip losses in Korea, while Japan eased 0.33%.

Hong Kong rose 0.57% as Beijing stepped in to boost stocks and cool the yuan, with Tencent up 1.27%. India outperformed with Nifty up 1.58% and Sensex up about 700 points on a Reliance-led rally, while Singapore's STI extended declines.

Sources Reuters, BigGo Finance, The Economic Times, Baystreet.ca, Sharecast.com, Bloomberg.com, Equitymaster, The Business Times

Commodities: Gas firms, metals fade

Oil swung on US-Iran talk uncertainty; Brent edged down 0.47% to 104.28 to extend its decline toward 103 while WTI was steady at 96.59. Natural gas rose 1.65% and added 2% intraday as output fell to a five-month low, with Goldman lifting forecasts.

Gold fell 0.93% to 3,932.50 below 4000 and silver fell 2.34% to a one-year low as the dollar and yields firmed, with silver back below 50. Copper fell 2.54% to a 10-week low as risk aversion gripped metals.

Sources Bloomberg.com, Financial Times, CNBC, Pipeline and Gas Journal, Natural Gas Intelligence, Kitco, Finimize, Forbes, Mining.com.au

Crypto holds despite outflows

Bitcoin rose 1.43% to 77,876 even as crypto ETFs lost $538 million on the day and more than $1 billion over two days led by BlackRock, with Binance outflows at $4.7 billion after the hack. Ether rose 1.90% despite six straight days of ETF selling, and Solana added 2.23%.

XRP rose 2.66% on 7,934 new wallets in a day and an upcoming ledger fix, outperforming Bitcoin. The resilience while funds left kept the broader crypto tape bid.

Sources CryptoRank, Quartz, CoinDesk, 24/7 Wall St., TradingView, The Defiant
