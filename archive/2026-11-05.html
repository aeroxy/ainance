What happened

Markets cheer divided Washington; yields and dollar slip

US stocks surged after Democrats flipped Congress, with the S&P up 1.60%, the Dow up 3.07% and the VIX down 19.44% to 16.78 on bets a divided government means fewer policy shocks. Treasuries advanced as the 10-year yield fell 6.2bp to 4.888% and the 2-year fell 3.5bp, while the dollar index slipped 0.35% to 101.51.

European stocks joined the rally, with the STOXX 600 up 1.82% to its highest since August as investors priced less tariff and fiscal uncertainty.

Sources NBC News, Reuters, Bloomberg, marketscreener.com, WSJ, AP News, Sharecast.com, The New York Times

Oil's war premium leaks out; gold and gas jump

Crude collapsed as midterms rewrote the Iran playbook: WTI fell 7.70% to 88.69 and Brent fell 8.06% to 95.25, both below key round numbers, on headlines that Vice President Vance and President Trump downplayed escalation and repudiated war. Energy was the worst US sector, down 4.71% on 2.0x volume.

The same dollar and yield drop lifted metals, with gold up 3.31% to 4070.70 and silver up 5.01% to 52.24, while natural gas rose 2.07% for a second straight gain as low output offset soft Chicago pricing.

Sources Politico, MarketWatch, Crude Oil Prices Today, NBC News, Axios, CNBC, AP News, Kitco, Anadolu Ajansı, Natural Gas Intelligence, Pipeline and Gas Journal

Tech splits: chips tumble, consumers lead

The rally was not uniform: semis fell 2.74% and weighed on global ETF returns as data-center and memory stocks tumbled on capex concerns, even as communications rose 3.48% and discretionary jumped 4.24%. The split showed tech is not one block, with Nvidia down 3.12% while leaders in consumer and comms led.

Defense and industrials also lagged, with aerospace and defense down 2.98% and industrials down 1.09% on worries a Democratic Congress curbs spending. Healthcare rose 3.07%, helped by Novo Nordisk's 4.95% jump after hiking its outlook on Wegovy pill sales, which lifted Europe.

Sources Reuters, Morningstar, Moomoo, 24/7 Wall St., Yahoo Finance, Investing.com, CNBC, AOL.com

Crypto rebounds with bitcoin; Asia diverges

Crypto tracked stocks higher despite three straight days of spot bitcoin ETF outflows of about $116M, with bitcoin up 2.93% back past 80150 and ether up 5.68% to reclaim its 200-day EMA as leverage hit five-month highs. Most big coins rose, but political tokens lagged, with TRUMP down 18.46% after the blue wave.

In Asia, Hong Kong and Shanghai rose 1.67% and 2.16% on US relief, while Tokyo fell 1.42% and Taiwan fell 0.83% on tech selling and Singapore was flat. The divergence left China-linked names like Tencent up 1.11% but trailing the broader Hong Kong gain.

Sources CryptoRank, CryptoSlate, The Block, Reuters, Coinpedia, MarketWatch, Yahoo Finance, Yahoo! Finance Canada, Aju Press, Breakingthenews.net, www.tokenpost.com, The Business Times
