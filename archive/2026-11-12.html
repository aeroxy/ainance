What happened

Yields and dollar jump, stocks shrug

The 10-year rose to 5.00% up 13.8bp and the 2-year rose to 4.75% up 20bp, with the dollar index up 0.68% to a 19-month high as hot October inflation kept Fed half-point hike chatter alive. Goolsbee called the report borderline disturbing and Goldman added another hike to its outlook.

Equities still rallied with S&P 500 up 1.3% to 7,618, Nasdaq up 1.6% to 25,460 and Dow up 0.47% as VIX fell 9.6% to 17.6, helped by a bond market close for Veterans Day. STOXX 600 rose 0.72% to 663 near a record, showing risk ignored the rates move for a session.

Sources Bloomberg.com, WSJ, Reuters, Business Insider, StoneX, CNBC, The New York Times, Eurasia Business News, Seeking Alpha

CoreWeave beat revives AI trade

CoreWeave rose 24.7% on 2.6 times average volume after Q3 revenue beat estimates and it raised its full-year outlook, easing debt pivots fears. The relief lifted semis 4.9% versus tech 3.2%, with Micron up 11.9%, SanDisk up 13.7%, Lumentum up 10.7% and optical names up 15% intraday.

Europe AI stocks rose 5.9%, 8 times the STOXX gain, on the same optimism, while Nvidia rose 2.9% and was indicated up near 4% pre-market despite scrutiny and a first call for Huang to step down on a China probe. The breadth argues for sector momentum rather than a single-name squeeze.

Sources Investor's Business Daily, Yahoo Finance, Stock Titan, Barron's, Bloomberg.com, Investing.com, Moomoo, MarketBeat, FourWeekMBA, The Hill

Taiwan and Europe surge, Hong Kong slips

Nikkei rose 1.3% to 61,249 and Taiwan rose 3.6% to 44,257 on chip demand, with SK Hynix up over 5%, while STOXX 600 neared a record on Siemens and CoreWeave optimism. Miners and tech led Europe as Germany bet on gas and lower oil helped margins.

Hang Seng fell 1.0% to 24,316 as tech and EV stocks slid, Shanghai rose 0.46% and STI rose 0.59%, leaving Hong Kong the laggard. The Hong Kong divergence shows high US rates weighing on China tech even as global AI names bounced.

Sources Reuters, Bloomberg.com, Yahoo Finance, TradingKey, Japan Today, BBN Times, BigGo Finance, TradingView, The Business Times

Oil firm, gas soft; crypto bounces with beta

WTI rose 0.37% to 86.36 and Brent rose 1.1% to 92.52 as progress stalled in US-Iran talks and Hormuz tensions lingered, but OPEC said output nears pre-Iran war levels and Trump said new Iran strikes are unlikely, capping upside. Gasoline held under $4 despite inflation, and heating oil talk of 70% spikes contrasted with ample supply signals.

Natural gas fell 1.7% to 3.24 on forecasts for normal US temperatures and EIA raising its November storage forecast after a warm start. Gold fell 0.87% to 3,995 as rate-hike bets built, silver rose 1.2% creating a divergence, and copper rose 0.6% with miners.

Bitcoin rose 1.2% to 74,671, ether rose 1.1% to 2,082 and solana rose 2.1% to 85 with Nasdaq strength, even as reports flagged continued Bitcoin ETF outflows of $2.22 billion and ether outflows that had only just shrunk. Zcash fell 5.0% below $1,000 despite a Grayscale push, Pepe fell 9.7% and Initia fell 49.6% after a $3.27 million exploit halted its chain.

Sources Barchart.com, Crude Oil Prices Today, Reuters, Al Jazeera, WSJ, American Journal of Transportation, IndexBox, Yahoo Finance, Crypto News, CoinDesk, TradingPedia, Cryptonews.net, NewsCord
